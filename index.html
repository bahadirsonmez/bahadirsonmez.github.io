About

I’m Bahadır, an iOS software engineer based in Berlin with eight years of experience building high-scale, consumer-facing apps in e-commerce and fintech.

I speed up daily development with AI coding agents and LLMs, while keeping rigorous manual code reviews and architectural consistency.

Since June 2026 I work independently, owning the full product and release lifecycle of iKeep, Bubbles, Ballance and HealthBar—from product thinking to App Store release and ongoing iteration.
